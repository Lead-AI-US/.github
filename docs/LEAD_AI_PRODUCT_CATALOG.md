# Lead.AI Product Catalog

## Overview

Lead.AI products are designed for businesses that need practical AI automation for leads, conversations, follow-up, customer intelligence, and trustworthy decision support.

## Lead.AI Platform

**Status:** In Development / MVP  
**Role:** Main Lead.AI SaaS platform/dashboard.  
**Value:** A central AI automation dashboard for managing leads, conversations, analytics, and workflow modules.

**Best for:** Small business owners, Sales teams, Customer support teams, Agencies, Startup operators

**Core features:**
- Dashboard
- Lead overview
- AI insights panel
- Product modules
- Conversation summary
- Usage analytics
- Automation status
- Integration placeholders
- Admin-ready architecture

**Recommended first demo:** Create a dashboard shell with lead, conversation, and automation module views.

## Lead.AI Business Audit

**Status:** MVP  
**Role:** AI-powered business automation audit tool.  
**Value:** A lead magnet and advisory tool that turns business pain points into an automation readiness score and 30-day roadmap.

**Best for:** Small business owners, Local service businesses, Consultants, Agencies, Startup founders

**Core features:**
- Business intake form
- Automation readiness score
- Lead response score
- Workflow gap analysis
- Recommended automation package
- 30-day roadmap
- PDF/report-ready output
- Lead capture CTA

**Recommended first demo:** Build the intake form, scoring rubric, and report-ready audit output first.
