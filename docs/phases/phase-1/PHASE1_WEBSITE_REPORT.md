**Last Verified:** 2026-08-08

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phase 1 transformed `lead-ai.us` into a modern, professional, high-trust AI SaaS portal focused on small business automation. 

### Key Deliverables Completed:
1. **Light/Dark Mode Theme Architecture:** Complete system with `next-themes`, zero flash on load, persistent `localStorage` preference, and accessible ThemeToggle component.
2. **Absolute Public-Pricing Rule:** 100% enforced. Zero dollar amounts ($), package prices, or financial promises exist anywhere in UI, code, metadata, JSON-LD, or data files.
3. **Business Automation Paths Section:** Replaced traditional pricing tables with 4 structured cards (Free AI Audit, Starter Setup, Growth System, Ongoing Support) driven by consultation CTAs.
4. **Lifecycle & Workflow Visualization:** Implemented 7-step customer progression (Capture → Understand → Qualify → Respond → Book/Handoff → Track → Improve) with explicit AI + Human control indicator (*"AI handles repeatable work. Humans stay in control."*).
5. **Local Business Use Cases:** Framed operational workflows for 7 sectors (Retail, Beauty, Local Services, Professional, Construction, E-commerce, Sales Teams).
6. **Verifiable Product Proof:** Removed all unverified social proof and replaced with live software demos (Business Audit, Hugging Face XAI demo, GitHub repositories).
7. **Secure Consultation Intake Bridge:** Built Consultation Modal with structured form intake (Name, Email, Business Type, Service Interest, Challenge description, Consent checkbox).
